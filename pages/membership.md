## How joining works

### Arrange a visit

Send us a message and we'll invite you to a Friday meeting.

### Come as a guest

We ask everyone to do this before joining, so you can see first hand what we do in our meetings and how we help each other. Each guest can visit twice within six months.

### Join the group

If your profession is open and Inspire is right for you, you become a member. We are all about commitment.

## Fees

Inspire is a not-for-profit organisation and doesn't look to make money from its fees.

| Fee | Amount | When |
|---|---|---|
| Joining fee | 10,000 THB | Covers your first year |
| Renewal fee | 5,000 THB | After one year of membership |
| Breakfast fee | 2,500 THB | Paid monthly |
| Events | Free | For members and partners |

Annual fees pay for our events during the year. The breakfast fee goes to the hotel for members' breakfasts. Guests who attend an event are charged a fee to cover its cost.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I try as a guest first before joining?

Yes, and we insist on it. We want you to see first hand what we are about, what we do in our meetings and how we help each other.

### How often can I come as a visitor?

We have a strict rule of two visits for each guest within a six-month period. That's ample time to see what we are about and whether it suits you.

### Are you allowed more than one of the same profession?

No. We are strictly a "one member per profession" group, and this is our firmest rule. We want you to succeed in a competition-free zone. Some members do offer the same services, but they have agreed to split those services between them.

### Where do I sign up?

[Send us a message](/contact-inspire/) to get started. We meet every Friday morning at 06:30, apart from official holidays, at the Mövenpick Sukhumvit Soi 15.
