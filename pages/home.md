## Who we are

Inspire is a long-established group of like-minded business people who come together to provide referrals, support, advice and guidance, so that each of us can grow our business. We come from a wide range of backgrounds, nationalities and industries.

[More about Inspire](/about-us-inspire/)

## Give. Grow. Gain.

### Give.

Share knowledge, offer support and create opportunities for fellow members. That builds the trust everything else rests on.

### Grow.

Use the group's tools, resources and connections to expand your horizons, from skill-building workshops to mentorship.

### Gain.

Genuine connections bring new opportunities, valuable insights and the support you need to thrive.

## Why join

### One member per profession

You grow your business with no competitor in the room.

### Weekly meetings

Meeting every Friday builds real trust between members.

### Trusted referrals

New business comes from colleagues who know your work.
