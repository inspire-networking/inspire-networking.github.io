This page explains what personal data this website collects, why, and the choices you have.

## Who we are

This website is run by [legal name of Inspire], which is responsible for your personal data under Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA). For any privacy question or request, email [privacy contact email].

## What we collect and why

### Messages you send us

When you use our contact form, we receive your name, your email address and your message. We use them to reply to you and to arrange a visit if you ask for one. The form is delivered by Web3Forms. We keep messages for [how long Inspire keeps messages].

### How the site is used

If you accept cookies, we use Google Analytics to see how people use the site, such as which pages they visit and how they found us. This data is kept for up to 14 months. If you decline, Google Analytics does not run.

### The map on our Contact page

The map comes from Google Maps. It loads only if you choose Show map, and Google may then set cookies and receive your IP address.

### Website hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not you accept cookies.

### Member profiles

Our Members and News pages show members' names, photos and business details. Members can ask us to change or remove their details at any time.

## Cookies

We set these cookies only if you accept them. You can change your choice at any time using [Cookie settings](#cookie-settings), which is also at the bottom of every page.

| Cookie | Set by | Purpose | Lasts |
|---|---|---|---|
| \_ga | Google Analytics | Tells one visitor from another | Up to 2 years |
| \_ga\_66YWLGK7TT | Google Analytics | Remembers the current visit | Up to 2 years |

We also store your cookie choice in your browser, so we don't need to ask you on every page.

## Who else handles your data

Google, GitHub and Web3Forms process data for the purposes above, and may do so outside Thailand. We don't sell your personal data.

## Your rights

Under the PDPA, you can ask us to give you access to your personal data, correct it or delete it. You can also object to or limit how we use it, and withdraw your consent at any time. To make a request, email [privacy contact email]. You can also complain to Thailand's Personal Data Protection Committee.
