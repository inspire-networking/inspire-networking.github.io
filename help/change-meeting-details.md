# Change meeting details

One file sets the meeting day, time and venue, and the social media links, across the whole site.

1. Open [settings.yml](https://github.com/inspire-networking/inspire-networking.github.io/blob/main/_data/settings.yml).
2. Click the **pencil icon** (Edit this file).
3. Change the words inside the quote marks, and keep the quote marks. For example:

   ```
   meeting_day: "Friday"
   meeting_start: "06:30"
   meeting_end: "09:30"
   ```

   Write the day in the singular, such as `Friday`. The site adds the "s" where it needs one.
4. Click **Commit changes...**, then **Commit changes**.

A few minutes later, check the footer of any page and the [Contact page](https://inspire-networking.github.io/contact-inspire/).

**One thing to change by hand:** the FAQ answer "Where do I sign up?" also mentions the time and venue. See [Change page wording](change-page-wording.md) and edit `membership.md`.

Leave the lines under "Technical values" alone.
