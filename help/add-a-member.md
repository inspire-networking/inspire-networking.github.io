# Add a member

You need the member's details, and ideally a photo and logo.

## 1. Upload the photo and logo

Follow [Add or replace a photo](add-or-replace-a-photo.md) and put both in `images/members`. Name them after the person, such as `jane-example.jpg` and `jane-example-logo.png`.

No photo yet? Skip this step. The site shows the Inspire logo until there is one.

## 2. Create the member's file

1. Open this link: **[Start a new member file](https://github.com/inspire-networking/inspire-networking.github.io/new/main?filename=_members/new-member.md&value=---%0Aname%3A%20%22Member%20name%22%0Abusiness%3A%20%22Business%20name%22%0Arole%3A%20%22%22%0Awebsite%3A%20%22https%3A%2F%2Fexample.com%22%0Aphoto%3A%20%22%22%0Alogo%3A%20%22%22%0A---%0AOne%20or%20two%20sentences%20about%20what%20the%20business%20does.%0A)**
2. In the file name box at the top, replace `new-member` with the person's name in lower case with hyphens, such as `jane-example`. Keep `.md` on the end.
3. Fill in the details, keeping the quote marks:

   | Line | What to put |
   |---|---|
   | `name` | The member's name |
   | `business` | The business name. Members are listed alphabetically by this. |
   | `role` | Their job title, or leave the quote marks empty |
   | `website` | The full address, starting `https://` |
   | `photo` | `/images/members/jane-example.jpg`, or leave empty |
   | `logo` | `/images/members/jane-example-logo.png`, or leave empty |

4. Replace the sentence under the second `---` with one or two sentences about the business. Quotes and colons are fine here.
5. Click **Commit changes...**, then **Commit changes**.

A few minutes later, check the [Members page](https://inspire-networking.github.io/inspire-members/).

To show them on the home page too, see [Choose members on Home](choose-members-on-home.md).

The About page's member figure doesn't update by itself. See [Change page wording](change-page-wording.md).
