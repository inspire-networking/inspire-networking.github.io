# Remove a member

1. Open the [members folder](https://github.com/inspire-networking/inspire-networking.github.io/tree/main/_members) and click the member's file.
2. Click the **three dots** (...) at the top right of the file, then **Delete file**.
3. Click **Commit changes...**, then **Commit changes**.
4. Open the [member images folder](https://github.com/inspire-networking/inspire-networking.github.io/tree/main/images/members). Delete their photo and logo the same way.

A few minutes later, check the [Members page](https://inspire-networking.github.io/inspire-members/).

If they were on the home page, choose someone to replace them. See [Choose members on Home](choose-members-on-home.md).

The About page's member figure doesn't update by itself. See [Change page wording](change-page-wording.md).
