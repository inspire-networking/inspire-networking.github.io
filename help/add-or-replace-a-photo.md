# Add or replace a photo

## Before you upload

- **Keep it under 2 MB and no wider than 2000 pixels.** The site can't shrink photos by itself, and large ones make it slow.
  - On Windows, open the photo in **Photos** and use **Resize image**.
  - On a Mac, open it in **Preview** and use **Tools**, then **Adjust Size**.
- **Name the file in lower case with hyphens,** with no spaces, such as `jane-example.jpg`.
- **Check you have permission to use it.** The photos here are public.

## Upload it

1. Open the right folder:
   - [Member photos and logos](https://github.com/inspire-networking/inspire-networking.github.io/tree/main/images/members)
   - [Article images](https://github.com/inspire-networking/inspire-networking.github.io/tree/main/images/news)
   - [Large page photos](https://github.com/inspire-networking/inspire-networking.github.io/tree/main/images/pages)
2. Click **Add file**, then **Upload files**.
3. Drag your file in.
4. Click **Commit changes**.

**To replace a photo,** upload a new one with exactly the same file name. It takes the old one's place.

**To use a new photo,** put its address in the member's or article's file, such as `/images/members/jane-example.jpg`.

Member photos are cropped to a 4:3 shape from near the top, so keep the face in the upper part of the picture.
