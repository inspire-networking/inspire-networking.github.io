# Change page wording

Use this to change text on Home, About, Membership, News, Contact or Privacy. It also covers the fees, the FAQ and the About figures.

1. Open the [pages folder](https://github.com/inspire-networking/inspire-networking.github.io/tree/main/pages).
2. Click the page's file, such as `about.md` or `membership.md`.
3. Click the **pencil icon** (Edit this file) at the top right of the file.
4. Change the words.
5. Click **Commit changes...**, then **Commit changes** in the box that appears.

A few minutes later, refresh the page on the website and check your change.

## How the file is laid out

- **The top part, between the two `---` lines, holds settings.** You can change words inside quote marks, but keep the quote marks. Leave anything else there alone.
- **A line starting `##` is a section heading, and `###` is a smaller heading.** You can reword them.
- **Leave an empty line between paragraphs.**
- **A link looks like** `[words people see](/membership/)`.

**Please don't add, remove or reorder `##` sections.** The page design relies on their order, so ask your support contact first.

## Common examples

**A fee:** in `membership.md`, change the amount in the table. Keep the `|` lines:

```
| Joining fee | 10,000 THB | Covers your first year |
```

**A figure:** in `about.md`, the large number is the `###` line:

```
### 25

**Members**
```

**An FAQ:** in `membership.md`, the question is the `###` line and the answer is underneath it. You can add one more question at the end of that section in the same way.

The meeting time in the green band and footer lives elsewhere. See [Change meeting details](change-meeting-details.md).
