Last week at inspire, [Kris Withouck](https://www.facebook.com/kris.withouck), managing director at Alcami gave his 10 minutes business presentation to inspire members and guests.

Alcami is a plastics and metals manufacturing company with facilities in Thailand and Philippines. Alcami specializes in plastic parts, including solutions such as CNC machining, thermoforming, injection molding, and sheet extrusion.

Founded in 2001, Alcami has since grown into a reliable subcontractor of manufacturing services for a wide range of industries, including:

- Electronics, computing and communications   
- Healthcare   
- Automotive   
- Food and beverage

No matter what your manufacturing project, Alcami is here to help. They have an in-house team of design engineers and incorporated post-production services to help them run your project smoothly from start to finish.

Alcami provides a broad range of assembly services, including both equipment assembly and machinery assembly. They can also undertake complex packaging operations once assembly is complete.

What differentiates Alcami from their competitors? They combine different production technologies allowing them to provide a turnkey solution for a variety of parts. They combine this with sourcing and logistics services, so their customers don’t need to worry about shipping and storage!

If you hear businesses in your networking complaining about their plastic parts supplier, such as they are too slow or not high enough quality, then please consider having a call with Kris, and making a switch to using Alcami.
